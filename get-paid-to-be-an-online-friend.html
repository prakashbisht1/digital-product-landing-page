5 Websites to Get Paid as an Online Friend ($15–$50/Hour)

Updated: 2026 • 6 min read • Platonic Remote Work

Can you actually get paid just by talking to someone online? Yes. As remote lifestyles expand, so does social isolation. Millions of people look for casual listeners, conversation buddies for language practice, and co-op gaming partners.

What Exactly Does a Virtual Friend Do?

Being an online friend is strictly platonic and non-romantic. Clients pay for designated blocks of your focused attention and time. The most common arrangements include:

  • Active Listening: Listening to someone vent about work or their week without judgment.
  • Casual Language Practice: Helping non-native speakers practice conversational English.
  • Co-op Gaming: Playing online titles (Roblox, Minecraft, Fortnite) while hanging out on voice chat.
  • Accountability Sessions: Sitting together on a muted video call during 45-minute focus or study blocks.

How Much Can You Make?

Most beginners start at $10 to $20 per hour ($0.15 to $0.30 per minute on per-minute apps). As you build positive ratings and specialize (e.g., specific game skills or professional conversation), you can increase your rate to $30 to $50+ per hour.

Top 5 Platforms to Get Paid as an Online Friend

1. Rent a Cyber Friend $0.10 – $1.00 / min

A completely remote platform dedicated strictly to digital connections via text and video calls. You set your own per-minute rate, and clients hire you for scheduled call blocks.

Best for: Purely virtual, flexible video chatting without in-person requests.

2. FriendPC $10 – $35 / hour

FriendPC offers diverse companionship categories, including virtual friends, gaming teammates, local guides, and daily motivational check-in texts. Payments are released quickly upon completing sessions.

Best for: Gamers and people offering text check-in packages.

3. Rent a Friend $10 – $50 / hour

Founded in 2009, Rent a Friend is the oldest and largest directory in the space. While it originally focused on local companionship, it now features a large virtual friend category.

Best for: High traffic volume and international client reach.

4. Fiverr $5 – $40 / gig

Fiverr lets you package your companionship as specific micro-services. Highly rated gigs include: "I will be your co-op gaming partner for 1 hour" or "I will provide 30 minutes of mock interview conversation."

Best for: Niche, structured service offerings with built-in search traffic.

5. Preply / Cambly $10 – $25 / hour

If you prefer structured conversations, language exchange platforms pay native English speakers to simply chat with learners worldwide. No formal teaching credentials are required for conversational tutors.

Best for: Guaranteed demand with clear boundaries.

⚠️ Essential Safety Rules
  • Never leave the platform for billing: Off-platform payments forfeit fraud protection and reveal personal banking details.
  • Use a stage name: Keep your full legal name, home address, and personal social accounts private.
  • Keep it platonic: Immediately cancel sessions if a user violates platonic boundaries or community guidelines.
